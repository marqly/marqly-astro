Marqly vs Notion for Bookmarks: Which Should You Use in 2026?

Feb 5, 2026

Many people wonder whether they should use Notion or a dedicated bookmark manager like Marqly. While both can store links, they serve fundamentally different purposes. Here's our detailed comparison.


Notion for Bookmarks: The Reality

Notion is a powerful all-in-one workspace, but using it for bookmarks has significant drawbacks. The Web Clipper saves pages to your workspace, but there's no automatic organization. You must manually add each link to databases, tag them, and maintain the structure. For heavy bookmark users, this becomes unsustainable.

Notion bookmark limitations include no AI-powered tagging, manual database management required, slower save process, no dedicated browser extension optimized for bookmarks, and overkill for simple link saving.


Marqly for Bookmarks: Purpose-Built

Marqly was designed specifically for bookmark management. Every feature exists to make saving, organizing, and finding bookmarks effortless.

Marqly advantages include AI-powered automatic tagging, one-click save with browser extension, smart boards that organize themselves, lightning-fast search, and dedicated reader mode for saved articles.


Use Cases Comparison

For research projects where you need to organize sources with notes and context, Notion might work if you're already using it for the project. But for general web browsing and saving content you find interesting, Marqly is far more efficient.

For teams collaborating on documents and databases with occasional link sharing, Notion makes sense. For individuals or teams focused on content curation and knowledge management, Marqly provides the specialized features you need.


The Hybrid Approach

Many users find success using both tools together. Marqly handles all bookmark management with its AI organization, then specific links are added to relevant Notion projects when needed for context.


Our Recommendation

If you primarily need bookmark management with intelligent organization, Marqly is the clear choice. Its AI-powered features eliminate manual work that makes Notion bookmarking tedious.

If you need a comprehensive workspace where bookmarks are just one small part, Notion might work, though you'll sacrifice bookmark-specific features.

Try Marqly free at marqly.com to experience the difference purpose-built bookmark management makes.
