Raindrop.io is, deservedly, the most popular bookmark manager around — especially after Pocket's shutdown sent millions looking for a home. It's polished, generous on the free tier, and saves everything. So why would you look at Marqly instead? One reason: **how you find things later.** This is an honest comparison of the two.

## At a glance

| | Raindrop.io | Marqly |
|---|---|---|
| Free tier | ✅ Generous | ✅ |
| Saves articles, PDFs, images, video | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manual collections & tags | ✅ Excellent | ✅ |
| Auto-tagging (AI) | Limited | ✅ |
| Search | **Keyword / full-text** | **Semantic (by meaning)** |
| AI summaries | ❌ | ✅ |
| Ask your library | ❌ | ✅ |
| Reader mode | ✅ | ✅ |
| Pro price | $3/mo | ~$7/mo |
| Maturity | Very mature | Newer, growing |

## Where Raindrop wins

Let's be fair — Raindrop is excellent at what it does:

- **The free tier is hard to beat.** If price is the deciding factor, Raindrop's free plan covers a lot.
- **It's mature and battle-tested**, with years of polish and a big community.
- **Manual organization is best-in-class** — nested collections, tags, and a clean visual grid. If you *enjoy* curating, Raindrop is a joy.
- **It saves every media type** reliably.

If you want a free, dependable, manually-organized bookmark library, Raindrop is the right pick, full stop.

## Where Marqly wins

The difference is the **retrieval model**.

- **Semantic search.** Raindrop's search matches keywords and full text. Marqly searches by *meaning* — describe a half-remembered article and it surfaces it even if your words aren't in the text. Once your library passes a few hundred items, this is the difference between finding things and giving up.
- **Auto-tagging.** Raindrop expects you to organize. Marqly's AI tags saves automatically, so the library structures itself.
- **Summaries + ask-your-library.** Marqly summarizes saves and lets you ask questions across everything you've saved — turning a bookmark list into a knowledge base.

The trade-off is honest: Marqly costs a bit more ($7 vs $3) and is younger.

## How to choose

- **Choose Raindrop** if you want free, you like organizing manually, and keyword/full-text search is enough for you.
- **Choose Marqly** if your saved library has outgrown folders and you want to find things by meaning without filing anything.

A simple test: open your current bookmark tool and try to find an article you saved months ago **without** remembering its title. If that's painful, semantic search is what you're missing.
