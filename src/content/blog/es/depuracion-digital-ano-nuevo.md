Aquí tienes una depuración digital que realmente puedes terminar: cinco fases — **pestañas, marcadores, lecturas pendientes, suscripciones y móvil** — con tiempo limitado para cada una, sumando unas dos horas en total. Sin retiros de 30 días ni exigencias imposibles de bandeja vacía. Y como una limpieza sin sistema vuelve a llenarse de caos en febrero, el último paso establece el hábito de captura que mantiene todo limpio para siempre.

Una regla antes de empezar: **archiva, no te angusties.** Cada decisión de esta lista es reversible: guardarás las cosas en un archivo local o en la nube con búsqueda semántica, no borrarás tu historial. La rapidez proviene de saber que nada valioso se pierde realmente.

## Paso 1: Las pestañas del navegador (20 minutos)

Open tabs are high-interest debt in your digital life: every one is an unfinished decision your brain keeps half-loaded.

1. **Count them.** Across all windows and devices.
2. **Save everything worth keeping in one action.** Don't evaluate tabs one by one. With Marqly's extension, the tab saver captures every open tab at once into your library, auto-tagged.
3. **Close every window.** Whatever mattered is captured; whatever wasn't captured demonstrably didn't matter.
4. **Adopt the ten-tab rule going forward.** When tabs pass ten, the overflow gets saved-and-closed. A tab is for *right now*; a save is for later.

## Paso 2: Los marcadores antiguos (30 minutos)

Years of "I'll need this someday" across browsers, none of it findable.

1. **Export from every browser.** Use the browser's bookmark manager HTML export.
2. **Prune only the obvious corpses.** Dead links, expired deals, docs for discontinued tools.
3. **Consolidate into one home.** Import the HTML files into a single bookmark manager — [check our import guide](/es/blog/exportar-marcadores-chrome). In Marqly, imports are auto-tagged and indexed for semantic search, eliminating the dreaded manual folder sorting. Read more on why [manual folder taxonomies fail](/es/blog/organizar-marcadores-navegador).
4. **Keep the browser bar for the daily dozen.** The handful of sites you open daily stay on the bar; everything else lives in the searchable manager.

## Paso 3: La lista de lectura pendiente (20 minutos)

The read-it-later queue with 400 articles is a guilt list, not a reading list. Same for the YouTube backlog — [see how to save YouTube videos efficiently](/es/blog/guardar-videos-youtube-ver-mas-tarde-2026).

1. **Declare bankruptcy on the bulk.** Anything saved more than six months ago that you don't specifically remember: archive it wholesale.
2. **Triage the recent hundred by headline.** Three quick piles: *actually want to read*, *only need the gist*, and *archive*.
3. **Let AI summaries clear the gist pile.** For pieces you need the core points of, Marqly generates fast AI summaries to settle thirty articles in one sitting.
4. **Cap the queue going forward.** Keep your active reading list under twenty items.

## Paso 4: Las suscripciones (30 minutos)

1. **Newsletters:** Search email for "unsubscribe" and keep only those you finished reading in the last month. Save key issues to your bookmark library before unsubscribing.
2. **Paid subscriptions:** Check bank and app store statements. Cancel anything not consciously used in the last 60 days.
3. **Unused accounts:** Delete dormant accounts when encountered to reduce exposure to future data breaches.

## Paso 5: El teléfono móvil (15 minutos)

1. **Home screen:** Keep daily apps on screen one; everything else lives in the app library/drawer.
2. **Delete unused apps:** Remove apps unopened in six months (except medical/emergency).
3. **Notifications default-off:** Allow notifications only for direct communications and urgent tools.

## Paso 6: Instala el sistema para que el desorden no regrese (15 minutos)

A declutter without a capture system is just a subscription to decluttering.

1. **One home for keepers.** Install a bookmark manager extension in every browser. One click saves the page, and AI tagging removes filing decisions.
2. **Trust retrieval, not memory.** With semantic search, describe what you remember to find it again. This simple habit builds a reliable [second brain](/es/blog/como-construir-un-segundo-cerebro-2026).
3. **A weekly ten-minute sweep.** Once a week, save-and-close overflow tabs and clear the queue.

## La lista de verificación en una sola pantalla

- [ ] Save all open tabs to your library and close windows
- [ ] Export bookmarks from all browsers to HTML
- [ ] Prune dead links and obvious duplicates
- [ ] Import everything into one searchable manager
- [ ] Archive read-later items older than 6 months
- [ ] Triage recent saves and AI-summarize gist articles
- [ ] Unsubscribe from unread newsletters
- [ ] Audit bank and app store subscriptions; cancel unused ones
- [ ] Clean home screen; turn off non-essential notifications
- [ ] Install one-click capture extension and schedule weekly 10-minute sweep

Dos horas, la mayor parte de forma automática y rápida.
