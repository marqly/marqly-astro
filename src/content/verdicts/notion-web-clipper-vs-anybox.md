Notion Web Clipper and Anybox serve fundamentally different saving workflows:

**Choose Notion Web Clipper if:**
- You organize research into relational databases with custom properties, formulas, and filters.
- You collaborate with team members or clients inside a shared digital workspace.
- You want bookmarks embedded directly into project documentation or personal wikis.
- You need access everywhere across web, Windows, Android, and Apple platforms.

**Choose Anybox if:**
- You exclusively use Apple devices (Mac, iPhone, iPad) and value native Swift responsiveness.
- You want an instant floating drawer, menubar launcher, and Raycast/Alfred quick-search shortcuts.
- You prefer local-first iCloud sync and automated offline webpage archives.
- You want simple bookmark filing without maintaining database schemas.

**The drawback of both:** Notion is heavy and slow for simple link retrieval; Anybox locks you into Apple hardware and requires manual folder/tag sorting. **Marqly** offers instant saving with AI auto-tagging, YouTube summaries, and natural-language semantic search that works everywhere.
