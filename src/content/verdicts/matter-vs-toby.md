Toby lives where work happens; Matter lives where reading happens. Toby turns the new-tab page into a visual command center — open tabs drag into collections, whole sessions stash and restore in one click, collections group into spaces shared with teammates or published as public links, and Toby AI names and sorts what you've got open. It's been at this since 2016 and remains the strongest team story in tab management, with SSO and org-wide collections on the Team plan. Matter ignores your browser almost entirely: it's an Apple-polished queue where saved articles arrive cleanly parsed, newsletters flow in via Gmail, YouTube and podcasts come transcribed, and the category's best text-to-speech reads it all to you.

**Choose Toby if:**

- Your tabs are working sets — projects, clients, recurring tools — that you reopen constantly
- You collaborate: shared spaces and no-account public links beat sending URL lists around
- One-click session restore would genuinely change your day

**Choose Matter if:**

- Your saved links are articles you mean to consume, ideally by ear
- You need content preserved and readable offline on iPhone and iPad, not bare URLs
- Highlights syncing to Notion, Obsidian, or Readwise feeds your notes practice

Bottom line: the caps reveal the fit. Toby's 60-tab free ceiling suits people who cycle through sessions, not hoarders building an archive; Matter's unlimited free library suits readers, but it has no Android app and no way to restore a browsing session. If losing the link would cost you a workflow, choose Toby. If losing the link would cost you the words, choose Matter.
