Karakeep and Anybox target different privacy and system architectures:

**Choose Karakeep if:**
- You want 100% self-hosted ownership over your data using Docker.
- You want automated AI tagging, OCR, and local-model semantic search.
- You need cross-platform apps for web, Android, and iOS.
- You prefer open-source software without vendor lock-in.

**Choose Anybox if:**
- You do not want to manage servers, Docker containers, or model weights.
- You want a native Swift interface integrated with macOS Menubar, Raycast, and Shortcuts.
- You want simple one-time pricing ($39.99 lifetime) with iCloud sync.
