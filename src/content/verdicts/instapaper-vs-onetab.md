The overlap here is a single moment: a browser groaning under open tabs, half of them articles you swear you will read. OneTab resolves the pressure — one click collapses everything into a local list, memory usage drops by up to 95% per its developer, and nothing leaves your machine. Instapaper resolves the intention — the articles themselves, not just their URLs, get saved, stripped to clean text, and synced offline to wherever you actually read.

**Choose Instapaper if:**

- The tabs are reading material; capturing article text beats keeping links that rot, and free unlimited saving removes any excuse
- You read on a phone, tablet, or Kindle — OneTab has no iOS app, no Android app, and no web account, so your list is desk-bound
- Highlights, folders, and an archive turn reading into something cumulative rather than a purge

**Choose OneTab if:**

- The tabs are everything else — dashboards, docs, half-finished errands — and you need them parked, not preserved
- Zero setup is sacred: no account, no limits, complete privacy, and instant restore of whole sessions
- The 2026 v2 additions (folders, search, stars, dark mode) provide all the structure your parked sessions require

Bottom line: use the test of regret. If losing the list tomorrow would cost you nothing but mild annoyance, OneTab is the perfect free tool for the job — over two million Chrome users agree. If losing it would mean losing things you meant to read, you were misusing a tab parker as a library. Send articles to Instapaper, park the rest, and let each tool do what it was built for.
