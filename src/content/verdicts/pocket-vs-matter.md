Mozilla retired Pocket in mid-2025, which makes Matter the living option — and for one specific audience, a genuine upgrade. Matter is a design-forward read-later app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and the web whose signature feature is the best text-to-speech in the category: human-like voices with word-by-word tracking that let you switch between reading and listening mid-article. Pocket's Listen feature pointed in this direction; Matter built the whole product around it.

**Choose Pocket if**

- You can't — the service shut off on July 8, 2025, and unexported data was deleted after that October.
- There is no legacy mode, archive, or successor app from Mozilla.

**Choose Matter if**

- You're in the Apple ecosystem and listen as much as you read: commutes, dishes, and walks turn your article backlog into narration.
- You want newsletters in your queue — they flow in via Gmail integration or a dedicated Matter email address — and YouTube videos and podcasts get time-synced transcripts you can highlight.
- The free tier is enough to start, with Premium at $60/year on the web adding HD voices, unlimited highlights and notes, and full-text search.

Two hard limits decide who shouldn't come here. First, there is no Android app, full stop — Pocket's Android users should route their export to Raindrop.io or Wallabag instead. Second, Matter has no public API and full-library export is more limited than rivals', so treat it as a reading queue rather than a permanent archive. Within those limits, the migration story is smooth: Matter imports Pocket files directly and picked up a large share of Pocket's diaspora. If your old ritual was pressing play on a saved-article playlist with headphones on, this successor will feel like progress rather than a compromise.
