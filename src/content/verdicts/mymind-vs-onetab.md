Honesty first: mymind and OneTab are not rivals, and any page telling you otherwise is stretching. OneTab exists for the moment your browser has forty tabs and your laptop fan is screaming — one click parks everything into a plain list, locally, for free. mymind exists for the tiny fraction of what you encounter that you actually want to keep: it turns saves into AI-tagged visual cards on a private canvas built to be browsed for years. Browser hygiene on one side, personal curation on the other.

**Choose OneTab if:**

- The problem in front of you is open tabs, memory usage, and clutter — not remembering things long-term.
- You refuse accounts and subscriptions: OneTab needs neither, and never transmits your URLs anywhere unless you share a list.
- Disposable is fine — parked tabs are things you will reopen this week or never miss.

**Choose mymind if:**

- You are collecting, not parking: inspiration, products, articles, and images that should still be findable next year.
- Visual search earns its subscription for you — describing a half-remembered image and having it surface is mymind's core trick.
- You want your collection on every device, with apps for web, Mac, iOS, and Android, where OneTab offers desktop browsers only.

The bottom line writes itself: these tools coexist rather than compete. Keep OneTab as the free relief valve for tab overload — while respecting its fragility, since uninstalling the extension deletes your list. Reserve mymind, and its $7.99+/month AI tiers, for the material that genuinely matters to you. The mistake is using either one for the other's job.
