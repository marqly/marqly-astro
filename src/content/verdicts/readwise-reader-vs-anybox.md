Readwise Reader and Anybox solve completely different stages of knowledge management:

**Choose Readwise Reader if:**
- You need a dedicated reading environment for RSS feeds, PDFs, newsletters, and EPUBs.
- You highlight extensively and want automated sync to Obsidian, Notion, or Roam.
- You want Ghostreader AI assistance for document summaries and translations.
- You read across non-Apple devices including web and Android.

**Choose Anybox if:**
- You primarily want a fast, native Mac/iOS bookmark utility rather than a reading app.
- You want Raycast and Alfred extension support for instant URL search and clipboard history.
- You want local-first privacy with iCloud synchronization and offline web archive copies.
- You prefer a $39.99 lifetime universal purchase over a $120/year subscription.

**The missing middle ground:** Reader can feel too heavy for everyday bookmarks, while Anybox lacks AI auto-tagging, semantic search, and cross-platform Android/Windows web access. **Marqly** provides instant semantic search, AI summaries, and web highlights in a fast, lightweight workspace.
