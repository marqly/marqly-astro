An 18-year-old commercial reading service meets an open-source upstart with the opposite theory of saving. Instapaper bets that less is more: a quiet queue, superb typography, manual folders, nothing automated. Karakeep — launched as Hoarder, renamed in 2025 — bets that AI should do the filing: every save gets auto-tagged and summarized by whatever model you plug in, archived in full against link rot, and retrieved later through semantic search, all running on your own server for free.

**Choose Instapaper if:**

- You want to read tonight, not administer a container — the hosted service needs nothing from you but a login
- Reading ergonomics matter: offline sync, tilt scrolling, speed reading, Kindle delivery on Premium, and a first-class iPhone app
- A stable, independently run business since 2008 is worth more to you than any feature list

**Choose Karakeep if:**

- Docker holds no fear and privacy is a priority — with a local Ollama model, even the AI tagging never leaves your machine
- Your saves go beyond articles: links, images, videos, and PDFs all land in one archived, searchable library
- You want modern retrieval free forever — semantic search and AI summaries that Instapaper simply does not build

Bottom line: this contrast is stark enough that most people know their answer immediately. Karakeep gives tinkerers a remarkable amount — 28,000+ GitHub stars, active releases, real AI — for the price of their own hardware and setup time. Instapaper gives everyone else a dependable, beautiful reading queue whose only real gaps are the paywalled search and the total absence of AI. Effort or money: pick which one you would rather spend.
