While built by the same company and bundled together on the Full subscription plan, Readwise and Readwise Reader address distinct phases of the knowledge workflow. Readwise is a **retention and resurfacing tool**: it ingests highlights from Kindle, Apple Books, Pocket, and articles, sending you a daily spaced-repetition review email and syncing notes to Notion, Obsidian, and Roam. Readwise Reader is an **ingestion and reading tool**: it provides a keyboard-driven triage inbox for RSS feeds, newsletters, saved articles, PDFs, and EPUBs, with Ghostreader AI to summarize and query documents.

**Choose Readwise (Lite plan) if:**

- Your primary reading happens on Kindle, Kobo, or physical books and you want your highlights automatically synced into an Obsidian vault or Notion workspace.
- You want the daily spaced repetition review habit to remember key insights from books you've read.
- You already have an article reading tool you love and don't need another reading inbox.
- You want the lower price tier ($4.49/mo billed annually) without paying for Reader.

**Choose Readwise Reader (Full plan) if:**

- You read heavy volumes of digital content: long-form web articles, Substack newsletters, academic PDFs, EPUBs, and YouTube video transcripts.
- You want a fast, keyboard-first reading environment with vim-style shortcuts and triage queues (Inbox, Later, Archive).
- You want Ghostreader AI assistance inside documents: term definitions, instant summaries, and section Q&A.
- You want all highlights made while reading to feed directly into your note-taking ecosystem.

**The missing piece:** neither product functions as an effortless AI bookmark organizer for general web resources, videos, or quick references. If you want your bookmarks automatically organized and searchable by meaning without maintaining an inbox queue, explore an AI-native bookmark manager like **Marqly**.
