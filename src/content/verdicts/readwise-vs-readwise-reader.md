Choose classic Readwise when you mainly want to collect and revisit highlights. Choose Reader when you also need an inbox for articles, PDFs, newsletters, and feeds. Reader is included in Readwise Full, while Lite covers classic Readwise. Compare the reading workflow first; these are related products with different jobs, not two independent subscriptions.

## Choose by the part of reading you want to improve

| Need | Classic Readwise | Reader |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revisit highlights after reading | Core job | Highlights can flow into classic Readwise |
| Read saved articles and documents | Use your existing reading surface | Reading inbox and document interface |
| Manage newsletters and feeds | Not its main role | Part of the reading workflow |
| Choose a subscription | Lite or Full | Included in Full |

As of October 6, 2026, [Readwise's official pricing](https://readwise.io/pricing) lists Full at $9.99 per month billed annually ($119.88 per year), or $12.99 billed monthly. It offers a 30-day trial, rather than a permanent free plan. The comparison table above separates tasks; it does not imply that a cheaper Lite plan includes Reader.

## Test the reading habit before migrating everything

Bring a few articles and highlights you already need. Check whether review sessions help you revisit useful passages and whether Reader's inbox fits your daily reading. Keep citation details and notes in your chosen research system. AI summaries are useful for triage, but a summary should not replace the underlying source when quoting or citing it.
