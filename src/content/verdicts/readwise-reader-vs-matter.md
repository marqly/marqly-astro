Two premium read-it-later apps, two definitions of premium. Readwise Reader means power: articles, RSS, newsletters, PDFs, ePubs, Twitter threads, and YouTube transcripts in one keyboard-driven inbox, Ghostreader AI answering questions against the text, and highlights flowing into Notion, Obsidian, and Roam with spaced-repetition review. Matter means polish: the category's finest reading design on iPhone and iPad, narration-grade voices that track word by word, and newsletters arriving through Gmail or a dedicated address.

**Choose Readwise Reader if:**

- Volume is the problem — a triage-first inbox with keyboard shortcuts is built for people processing dozens of items daily
- Notes are the output; the Readwise highlight loop into Notion, Obsidian, and Roam is the strongest reason the subscription exists
- You need Android, a documented API, or ePub support — Matter offers none of the three

**Choose Matter if:**

- You listen more than you read: HD text-to-speech with word tracking makes commutes and workouts genuine reading time
- Apple hardware is settled fact — iPhone, iPad with Pencil highlighting, and Mac get Matter's full attention
- $60/year on the web (with a workable free tier) fits better than $119.88 with no free fallback

Bottom line: Reader is the maximal tool and charges accordingly — twice Matter's web price, with a dense UI and a learning curve casual readers will feel. Matter is narrower on purpose, and inside its Apple-plus-audio lane it is the more pleasant product. Both sync highlights onward (Matter exports to Notion, Obsidian, and Readwise itself), so the note-taking argument only forces Reader if you want the full review loop. Match the app to your platform and your dominant mode, and neither will disappoint.
