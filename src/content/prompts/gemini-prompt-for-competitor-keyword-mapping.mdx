## How to use this prompt
1. Copy the full prompt above.
2. Open your preferred AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, etc.).
3. Replace every bracketed placeholder such as [TOPIC], [AUDIENCE], or [TONE] with your own details.
4. Paste the customized prompt and run it.
5. Iterate on the output by adding constraints or examples.

## Why this prompt works
It grounds the AI in a target audience, keyword, and deliverable, making the output actionable and search-intent driven.
