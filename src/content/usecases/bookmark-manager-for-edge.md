Edge deserves better than hand-me-down extensions, and bookmark tools are usually the worst offenders — Chrome builds ported over as an afterthought, if they're available at all. Marqly ships a dedicated add-on on the [Microsoft Edge Add-ons store](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/marqly-%E2%80%93-the-ultimate-boo/gojjglmdginjjpgajdnobmnkmcogngok): an AI bookmark manager that saves any page in one click, tags and summarizes it automatically, and finds it later by meaning — "that article about pricing psychology" — instead of making you remember titles or dig through favorites folders.

## The gap in Edge favorites

Edge's favorites system is the classic browser design: a folder tree, a toolbar bar, and search that matches titles and URLs. It captures nothing about what a page says, so retrieval depends entirely on you remembering what you named a thing and where you filed it. Past a few hundred favorites, that stops working — not because you're disorganized, but because folder taxonomies always decay.

The sync boundary is the other wall. Edge favorites sync through your Microsoft account, which is convenient right up until you touch a browser that isn't Edge. Chrome on the family laptop, Safari on your iPhone — your favorites don't follow. For anyone who lives in Edge at work but somewhere else at home, that's a daily paper cut.

## A dedicated add-on, installed the normal way

Because Marqly publishes directly to Microsoft's store, installation is native: open the listing, click Get, done. No enabling extensions from other stores, no wondering whether an unported Chrome build will behave.

Once installed, the toolbar button saves the current page in one click, with optional tags at save time. The tab saver collapses an entire research session — every open tab — into your library in one action. The side-panel library lets you browse and search your saves right beside the page you're on, so checking what you've already collected never costs you your place.

The AI layer is what separates this from a favorites bar with better UI. Every save is auto-tagged — no filing, ever — and articles get AI summaries you can triage in seconds: skim the summary, decide whether the piece deserves a full read, move on. A backlog review that used to mean reopening thirty pages becomes a ten-minute pass in the side panel. The highlighter lets you mark text on any page in six colors with notes; highlights persist when you revisit and sync to your library. On YouTube, Marqly adds an AI card with summary, transcript, and (on Pro) chat tabs, plus a bookmark button that saves videos with their transcripts attached. And semantic search ties it together, matching your descriptions against titles, content, highlights, and transcripts — here's [how AI bookmark search works](/blog/how-to-search-bookmarks-with-ai) in practice.

## Move your favorites in, keep your other browsers too

Edge exports favorites to a standard bookmark HTML file, and Marqly imports it directly — the process is identical to the Chrome flow covered in [our bookmark import guide](/blog/how-to-import-chrome-bookmarks-to-ai). Import once, and the AI tags your entire favorites history; the folder tree you stopped maintaining in 2023 becomes a searchable library the same afternoon. Boards then give the keepers somewhere to live: group related links and highlights by project, and share any board as a public page that colleagues can open without an account.

From then on, saves stop being browser property. Your library lives in your Marqly account and [syncs across devices](/faq/can-i-sync-across-devices): the same saves appear in Chrome, Firefox, and Safari through their extensions, anywhere through the web app at app.marqly.com, and on iPhone through the iOS app. Save a link in Edge at your desk at 4 p.m., open it from your phone on the train at 6.

## Getting started

1. Open the [Marqly add-on on Microsoft Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/marqly-%E2%80%93-the-ultimate-boo/gojjglmdginjjpgajdnobmnkmcogngok) and click Get. (More detail: [does Marqly work on Edge?](/faq/does-marqly-work-on-edge))
2. Sign up free at [app.marqly.com](https://app.marqly.com) — no card required. Pro, when you want it, runs $72 a year (about $6 a month billed annually) or $9 monthly, with a 7-day free trial; it unlocks features like AI Q&A over your saves.
3. Export your Edge favorites to HTML and import them, so the AI can organize the backlog.
4. Save your next interesting page with one click and try describing it to search a day later.
