Sales research is the same job on repeat: before every discovery call you're digging through funding announcements, pricing pages, product changelogs, analyst reports, and whatever the champion posted last week. The links pile up across browser tabs, notes apps, and a bookmarks bar you stopped trusting months ago. When the deal finally moves, the article you need is somewhere — you just can't say where.

Marqly fixes the storage half of that job. It's an AI bookmark manager: one click saves any page, AI tags it automatically, and semantic search finds it later from whatever you actually remember — "that article about their CFO leaving" — no exact title required.

## Capture account intel without leaving the page

Research sessions are messy by nature. You start on a prospect's pricing page, end up fifteen tabs deep in competitor comparisons and a podcast the CEO did last quarter. Marqly's extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari) gives you three ways to capture all of it:

- **One-click save** from the toolbar for the page you're on, with tags if you want them.
- **Tab saver** to save every open tab at once when the session's over — see [how to save all your open tabs](/faq/how-do-i-save-all-my-open-tabs) for the mechanics.
- **Side-panel library** to check what you've already saved on an account without switching away from the page you're reading.

The point is that capture costs nothing, so you actually do it. No "I'll remember this URL" — you won't.

## Let AI do the filing

The reason most reps abandon bookmark folders is that filing takes judgment, and judgment takes time you'd rather spend selling. Which folder does an article about a prospect's Series C go in — the account folder, the "funding news" folder, the industry folder? [Folder filing breaks down](/blog/stop-organizing-bookmarks-folders-obsolete) precisely because everything belongs in more than one place.

Marqly skips the question. Every save gets AI tags automatically, so a funding announcement is findable by company, by topic, and by whatever else it's about — with zero filing on your end. And when an analyst report lands in your queue, the AI summary tells you whether it deserves twenty minutes or a skim, before you commit either.

## One board per target account

Tags handle the automatic layer. For deliberate curation, use boards: group links and highlights into a board per account — the funding news, the pricing page, case studies from their competitors, the conference talk their VP gave. Everything about the deal in one view.

When you want someone else to see the trail — a colleague inheriting the account, a manager prepping for an exec call — share the board as a public page. Anyone can view it without signing up. It's read-only sharing, not a shared workspace, but for "here's everything I've gathered on this account," it does the job. For more on structuring a system like this, see [how to organize bookmarks](/blog/how-to-organize-bookmarks).

## Find the fact when the deal needs it

Saving is only half the job; retrieval under pressure is the other half. Marqly's semantic search works across titles, page content, highlights, and video transcripts — so you search by meaning, in your own words. "The article where they announced the Europe expansion" finds the right save even if the headline never used the word "expansion."

Two features make retrieval sharper for sales work:

- **Highlighter.** Select the number that matters in a market report — the ACV benchmark, the churn stat — highlight it in one of six colors, and add a note. Highlights sync to your library and are themselves searchable. Here's [how highlighting works on any website](/blog/how-to-highlight-text-on-any-website).
- **YouTube saves with transcripts.** When a prospect's exec gives a conference talk, save the video and the transcript comes attached. Later, search finds what they said on stage, not just the video's title.

3. Import your existing browser bookmarks (standard HTML export) so the old pile gets AI tags too.
4. Start a board for your top three accounts and save into them for a week.

The free tier is enough to test the workflow. Pro is $72/year (about $6/month billed annually) or $9/month, with a 3-day trial, and adds things like chat with your saves — asking questions answered from your own research pile.

## Is Marqly the right fit?

Honest scope check: Marqly is a personal research library, not a sales platform. There's no CRM integration (no public API), no team workspace, no shared editing, and no Android app (the web app works in Android browsers). If your team needs account research living inside Salesforce or a shared wiki, Marqly won't replace that layer.

What it replaces is the tab chaos underneath it — the forty open tabs, the self-DMs, the bookmark folders you never reopen. If your research currently lives nowhere in particular, this is the fix. Try Marqly free at [app.marqly.com](https://app.marqly.com).
