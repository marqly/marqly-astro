Boards are Marqly's collections: they group related links and highlights around one topic — a project, a course, a reading list, a research thread. Instead of everything sitting in one flat library, a board gives a subject its own page holding everything you've gathered on it. Any board can also be shared as a public page that anyone can view without signing up.

## What goes on a board

Two kinds of things: **links** — articles, videos, and pages you've saved — and **highlights**, the exact passages you marked with [Marqly's highlighter](/faq/how-do-i-highlight-text-on-a-website). That combination is what makes boards useful for real research: a board holds both the sources and the specific quotes that made those sources worth keeping, side by side.

## Automatic filing, deliberate collections

Marqly's AI auto-tagging files every save for you, so your library organizes itself without folders. Boards sit on top of that as the deliberate layer: curation you do on purpose, for the handful of topics you're actively working on. You don't need a board for everything you save — most saves just live in the library, findable by search. Boards are for the projects where you want the material assembled in one place.

## Share it or keep it private

Boards are private by default. When a collection is worth showing someone — a resource list, a reading guide, research for a collaborator — [share the board publicly](/faq/how-do-i-share-a-board-publicly): it becomes a public page viewable by anyone with the link, no Marqly account needed. You can browse boards other people have shared at [app.marqly.com/discover](https://app.marqly.com/discover) to see what finished ones look like.
