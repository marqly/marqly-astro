Open Marqly's side panel from the extension and your library appears alongside the page you're on. Browse your saves, run a search, or pull up something you kept last month — all without leaving the current tab or losing your place in what you're reading. It's the difference between glancing at your library and context-switching to it.

## Your library without the tab switch

The moment the side panel is built for: you're reading something new and remember you saved a related piece. Normally that means a new tab, a hunt through the library, and a lost train of thought. With the side panel, the library slides in next to the article — you check the saved piece, compare, and keep reading where you left off.

## Search from wherever you are

Search in the side panel works the way Marqly search works everywhere: [by meaning, not just keywords](/faq/how-do-i-search-my-bookmarks-by-meaning). Describe what you remember — "the post about email deliverability" — and the save comes back even if its title says something entirely different. For the full picture of what that search can do, see [how to search bookmarks with AI](/blog/how-to-search-bookmarks-with-ai).

## When it earns its place

- **Research sessions** — keep your gathered sources in view while you work through a new one.
- **Writing** — pull up the article you're citing without burying your draft under tabs.
- **Fact-checking yourself** — "didn't I save something that contradicts this?" is now a five-second question.

The side panel ships with the Marqly extension — [install it for Chrome](/faq/how-do-i-install-the-chrome-extension), or grab the Edge, Firefox, or Safari version.
