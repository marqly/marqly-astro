There are no public Marqly coupon codes. The built-in discount is annual billing: Pro costs $48 per year versus $8 per month, so paying annually saves you about 50% compared to twelve monthly payments. Any third-party "coupon" site claiming to have working Marqly promo codes is guessing at best — those codes don't exist, and some of those sites exist mainly to harvest clicks.

## The annual plan is the discount

Run the numbers:

- Monthly: $8 × 12 = $96 per year
- Annual: $48 per year, about $4 per month

That's the deepest discount available, it's permanent, and it requires no code. Full details in [how much does Marqly cost](/faq/how-much-does-marqly-cost) and on the [pricing section](/#pricing) of the homepage.

## About coupon sites

If you search "Marqly promo code," you'll find aggregator sites listing codes like "SAVE20" with fake success rates. None of these are real. Coupon aggregators auto-generate pages for nearly every SaaS product to capture search traffic, whether or not the product has ever issued a code. Trying them wastes your time, and entering payment details on lookalike checkout pages linked from those sites is a genuine risk. The only place to buy Marqly Pro is [app.marqly.com](https://app.marqly.com).

## Ways to pay less that actually work

- **Bill annually.** $48/yr instead of $96/yr — the 50% saving mentioned above.
- **Use the 7-day free trial** before paying anything, so you never pay for a product you won't use.
- **Stay on the free tier** if the core save, auto-tag, and search experience covers your needs — it has no time limit and needs no card.

## Student pricing?

There's no published student discount today, though it never hurts to email support and ask — see [is there a student discount](/faq/is-there-a-student-discount) for the honest answer.
