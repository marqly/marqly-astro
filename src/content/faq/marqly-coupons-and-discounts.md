Marqly runs exactly one promo code: **STANDING49**, the standing first-year offer — $49 for your first year of annual billing instead of $72, then $72/year. Beyond that, the built-in discount is annual billing: Pro costs $72 per year versus $9 per month, so paying annually saves you about 33% compared to twelve monthly payments. Any third-party "coupon" site claiming to have other working Marqly promo codes is guessing at best — those codes don't exist, and some of those sites exist mainly to harvest clicks.

## The annual plan is the discount

Run the numbers:

- Monthly: $9 × 12 = $108 per year
- Annual: $72 per year, about $6 per month
- Annual, first year with STANDING49: $49 — 31% off the annual list price for year one

Annual billing is the permanent saving; the first-year code just makes year one cheaper still. Full details in [how much does Marqly cost](/faq/how-much-does-marqly-cost) and on the [pricing section](/#pricing) of the homepage.

## About coupon sites

If you search "Marqly promo code," you'll find aggregator sites listing codes like "SAVE20" with fake success rates. None of these are real — the only code the checkout accepts is STANDING49, applied automatically on annual billing. Coupon aggregators auto-generate pages for nearly every SaaS product to capture search traffic, whether or not the product has ever issued a code. Trying them wastes your time, and entering payment details on lookalike checkout pages linked from those sites is a genuine risk. The only place to buy Marqly Pro is [app.marqly.com](https://app.marqly.com).

## Ways to pay less that actually work

- **Bill annually.** $72/yr instead of $108/yr — the 33% saving mentioned above.
- **Use STANDING49 in your first year.** $49 instead of $72 — 31% off your first year.
- **Verify a student email.** Verified students get Pro for $48 in their first year instead of $72 — see below.
- **Stay on the free tier** if the core save, auto-tag, and search experience covers your needs — it has no time limit and needs no card.

## Student pricing?

Yes — and it beats the standing offer. Verified students get Marqly Pro for $48 in their first year instead of $72, confirmed automatically with a university email address at checkout (no waiting on a manual review). Full details, including what to do if your university email isn't recognized automatically, are in [is there a student discount](/faq/is-there-a-student-discount).
