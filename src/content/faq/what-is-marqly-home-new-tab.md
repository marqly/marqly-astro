Marqly Home replaces the blank New Tab page with a personal browser workspace. It combines search, quick links, notes, to-dos, weather, backgrounds, and saved browsing sessions, so the things you reach for every day are ready before you open another site.

## Quick links that work your way

Add favorite sites, drag them into the order you want, and group them in folders. A customizable dock and background let the page feel personal without turning it into another complicated dashboard.

## Notes, to-dos, and desktop stickies

Open draggable, resizable windows for notes and to-dos, or leave a sticky directly on the desktop. Marqly Home uses a familiar desktop-style layout so temporary context stays visible while you browse.

## Search and saved sessions

Search your Marqly library from the New Tab page instead of opening the full web app. Saved sessions also let you return to a group of tabs for a project or research task. Learn more about [saving all open tabs](/faq/how-do-i-save-all-my-open-tabs).

Marqly Home is currently available in Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox. It is not currently available in Safari. See [every extension feature by browser](/extension#availability).
