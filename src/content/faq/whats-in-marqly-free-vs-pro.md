Marqly Free is the core bookmark manager: save links from your browser, get AI auto-tagging on every save, and find anything with semantic search. Pro unlocks the chat features — asking questions answered from your saved content, and chatting with YouTube videos through their transcripts. Pro costs $79 per year or $9 per month, with a 7-day free trial to test it first.

## What Free includes

The free tier is the everyday save-tag-search loop. You save a page in one click, AI tags it automatically so you never file anything by hand, and semantic search finds saves by meaning — describe what you remember ("that article comparing note apps") instead of guessing the exact title. No card is required to sign up at [app.marqly.com](https://app.marqly.com), and the plan doesn't expire.

## What Pro unlocks

Pro adds two chat capabilities on top of everything in Free:

- **Chat with your saves** — ask a question and get an answer pulled from the content you've saved, not from the open web. Useful when you've saved twenty articles on a topic and want the takeaway without rereading them.
- **Chat with YouTube videos** — on any YouTube watch page, the Chat tab answers questions from the video's transcript, so you can query a 40-minute video instead of scrubbing through it.

## Which one do you need?

If your workflow is "save it now, find it later," Free covers you. If you treat your saves as a knowledge base you want to question — research, competitive analysis, learning from long videos — Pro is where that happens. Pricing details are on the [pricing section](/#pricing), and the [cost breakdown](/faq/how-much-does-marqly-cost) explains why annual billing is the better deal.

## Try before you pay

The 7-day Pro trial lets you test both chat features on your real library. If you don't subscribe afterwards, you keep the free tier and all your saves — see [what happens when the trial ends](/faq/what-happens-when-my-trial-ends).
