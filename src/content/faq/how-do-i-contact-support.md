Email support@marqly.com — that's the direct line for account questions, billing issues, bug reports, feature requests, and partnership inquiries. For self-serve answers, the help center at [help.marqly.com](https://help.marqly.com) covers setup and how-to guides. Those are the two support channels: the help center when you'd rather find the answer yourself, email when the question needs a human.

## What to put in your email

Three things speed everything up: the email address on your Marqly account, what you were trying to do, and what happened instead. Screenshots help more than descriptions. If it's a bug — something broken rather than something confusing — there's a short checklist worth following in [how to report a bug](/faq/how-do-i-report-a-bug), including which version details to attach.

## Billing questions go to the same place

Subscription changes, payment problems, and refund requests are all handled through support@marqly.com. Two of the most common are already written up: [how to cancel your subscription](/faq/how-do-i-cancel-my-subscription) and [can I get a refund](/faq/can-i-get-a-refund). Include the account email in billing messages — it's how your subscription gets located.

## Feature requests are welcome

Support isn't just for problems. If Marqly almost fits your workflow but not quite, a two-line email describing the gap genuinely informs what gets built next. Concrete beats abstract: "I save 30 papers a week and need X" is far more useful than "you should add X."

## Where Marqly posts updates

Product updates land on X at [@getmarqly](https://x.com/getmarqly). That's the channel to watch for new features and changes — support email is for conversations, not announcements.

A practical note on email: the quality of the first message sets the pace of the whole exchange. A well-written one — account email, context, screenshot — usually gets everything resolved in a single round trip instead of three.
