Any Marqly board can be shared as a public page. Share the board, send the link, and anyone can view it in their browser — no Marqly account, no signup, nothing to install on their end. It's the simplest way to hand someone a curated collection of links and highlights instead of a wall of pasted URLs in a chat message.

## What viewers see

A public board renders as a clean page of the collection: the links you grouped and the [highlights](/faq/how-do-i-highlight-text-on-a-website) you added to it. Because viewing requires no signup, there's no wall between your audience and the content — the link works for a classmate, a client, or a stranger on the internet equally well. Only boards you explicitly share are public; the rest of your library stays private.

## Where public boards show up

Marqly has a public boards directory at [app.marqly.com/discover](https://app.marqly.com/discover). Browsing it is a good way to see what strong boards look like before you build your own — and it's where shared collections get found by people beyond the folks you sent the link to.

## What boards are good for sharing

- **Resource lists** — everything worth reading on a topic, assembled once instead of re-sent forever.
- **Reading guides** — a course syllabus, an onboarding list, a "start here" for your niche.
- **Research handoffs** — sources plus the exact highlighted passages that matter, so the recipient skips the digging you already did.
