Yes. Marqly stores your bookmarks, highlights, and notes in your private account, visible only to you unless you explicitly share a board publicly. Your data is not sold to anyone — the business runs on Pro subscriptions, not advertising, so there's no incentive to monetize your browsing habits. The [privacy policy](/privacypolicy) spells out exactly what's collected and how it's used.

## The business model is the safety argument

The most reliable way to judge a free-tier product is to ask how it makes money. Ad-funded tools profit from profiling you; Marqly profits when users pay $72/yr for Pro features. That alignment means the product gets better by being more useful, not by extracting more data. More on this in [does Marqly sell my data](/faq/does-marqly-sell-my-data).

## What's private by default

Everything you save is private by default. The one deliberate exception: if you choose to share a board as a public page, that specific board becomes viewable by anyone with the link — that's the feature working as intended, and it only happens when you turn it on. Individual saves, highlights, notes, and your search history are never public.

## Practical account safety

- Your library syncs through your account, so a lost laptop doesn't mean lost bookmarks — sign in from another device and everything's there.
- You stay in control of the exit: you can [export your data](/faq/can-i-export-my-data) or delete your account whenever you want.
- Anything unclear? Email **support@marqly.com** — privacy questions get real answers, not boilerplate.
