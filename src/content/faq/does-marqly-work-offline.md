Yes — with a Pro plan, in the **web app and the iOS app**. Marking a bookmark "Save Offline" caches its clean article copy on that device, so it opens on a plane, a subway, or anywhere the signal dies. Everything else about Marqly stays online: saving new links, searching the library, and the AI features all need a connection.

## Exactly what works offline

- **Cached articles, on the device where you cached them.** Open the saved page and its reader view comes from the local cache — no network required.
- **A "Saved Offline" view** in the library shows what is genuinely available on *this* device right now, and search inside that view only matches what is truly cached.
- **Settings shows the cache size** so you can cap it and clear it when you're done.

## The honest limits

- **It's a per-device cache, not a synced offline library.** Nothing is stored offline on Marqly's servers, and marking a page for offline on your laptop doesn't download it to your phone. Each device keeps its own copies.
- **Videos are not cached, and big pages can be skipped** — images over 2 MB and pages over 5 MB may not make it into the cache.
- **The free plan has no offline reading** — it's one of the Pro features, like auto-tagging, summaries, and semantic search.
- **The Android app doesn't do article downloads yet.** On an Android device, the web app's offline cache is the way to read saves without a connection.
- **This isn't an offline-first reader.** Instapaper, Matter, and Wallabag sync an entire reading queue to your devices automatically; Marqly caches the pages *you* choose. If guaranteed auto-download of your whole backlog is your core habit, weigh that difference before switching.

## Why the library itself stays online

Because your bookmarks, highlights, and summaries live in your account, nothing is trapped in a single browser profile. Save an article from Chrome at your desk, and it's already in the [web app](https://app.marqly.com) and on your iPhone. Offline copies are conveniences layered on top; they're never the only copy. See [can I sync across devices](/faq/can-i-sync-across-devices) for how the account model works.

## If you need a permanent local copy

Offline cache entries live on the device — clear them, wipe the device, or let the plan lapse, and they're gone. For keepsakes, use Marqly's save-as-PDF feature: it captures any page as a clean PDF matching the on-screen layout, processed locally in Chrome and Edge. The PDF is a regular file that opens anywhere, with or without a plan. Details in [how do I save a page as PDF](/faq/how-do-i-save-a-page-as-pdf).

## The short version

Pro + web app or iPhone app + a page you marked for offline = readable with no connection, on that device. Everything else — the full library, search, AI, saving — follows your account online, which is exactly what makes it reachable from every signed-in device.
