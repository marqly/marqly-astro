Install Marqly from the Chrome Web Store: open the Marqly listing, click "Add to Chrome," and confirm. Then pin the extension so it's always visible — click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and hit the pin next to Marqly. From then on, saving any page is one click on the Marqly icon. Signing up is free and no credit card is required.

## Step by step

1. Open the [Marqly listing on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/marqly-all-in-one-bookmar/kcadneobjofkppmekgadodnaojoehemc).
2. Click **Add to Chrome**, then **Add extension** in the confirmation dialog.
3. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the right end of Chrome's toolbar.
4. Click the **pin** next to Marqly so the icon stays on your toolbar.
5. Sign in or create a free account at [app.marqly.com](https://app.marqly.com) — no card needed.

Pinning matters more than it sounds: an unpinned extension hides behind the puzzle menu, and a save button you can't see is a save button you won't use.

## What you get after installing

The extension is the core of Marqly, and it does more than save the current tab:

- **One-click save** from the toolbar, with optional tags at save time
- **AI auto-tagging** on every save — no folders, no manual filing
- **Tab saver** — capture all open tabs at once when a research session sprawls
- **Side panel** — browse and search your library without leaving the page
- **YouTube AI card** on every watch page, with AI summary, chat, and transcript tabs

Everything you save lands in your account, so it's also available in the web app and the iOS app.

## Already have years of Chrome bookmarks?

You don't have to start from zero. Export your existing bookmarks from Chrome and import them into Marqly — AI tags the whole backlog automatically.
