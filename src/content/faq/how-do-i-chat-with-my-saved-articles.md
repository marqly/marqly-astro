Ask Marqly a question in plain language and get an answer grounded in your saved content — the articles, highlights, and video transcripts already in your library. Instead of re-opening five saves to hunt down one figure or half-remembered argument, you ask for it and the answer is drawn from what you actually kept. Chat with your saves is a Marqly Pro feature.

## Answers come from your library

The point of chatting with your saves is grounding: answers are based on the content you saved, not generic web knowledge. You curated the sources — the answer reflects *your* reading. That's what separates this from asking a general chatbot, which has never seen the specific articles you trusted enough to keep. As with any AI output, [verify critical facts against the source](/faq/how-accurate-are-ai-summaries) before you rely on them.

## Chat and search are different tools

[Semantic search](/faq/how-do-i-search-my-bookmarks-by-meaning) finds *the save* — "that article about pricing psychology." Chat answers *the question* — "what did that article say charm pricing does to perceived quality?" Use search when you want to open and read something; use chat when you want the specific answer buried inside things you've already read. Together they make the library a working reference instead of a pile.

## What you need

Chat with your saves requires Marqly Pro: $48/year (about $4/month billed annually) or $8/month, with a 7-day free trial. See [what's in Marqly free vs Pro](/faq/whats-in-marqly-free-vs-pro) for the split.
