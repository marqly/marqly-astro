Use Marqly's tab saver to save all your open tabs at once. Every tab lands in your Marqly library, where AI auto-tagging files each page automatically — no bookmarking them one by one, no dragging anything into folders. A 30-tab research session becomes a two-second action, and the window can finally close.

## Tab hoarding is a filing problem

Tabs stay open for one reason: closing them feels like losing them, and saving them properly — one at a time, each into the right folder — takes longer than anyone will actually spend. The tab saver removes both halves of the problem. Capture is one action for the whole set, and filing is handled by [AI auto-tagging](/faq/how-does-ai-auto-tagging-work), so there's no organizing debt waiting for you afterward.

## Closed, but not lost

Once saved, those pages are more findable than they ever were as tabs. Marqly's [semantic search](/faq/how-do-i-search-my-bookmarks-by-meaning) retrieves saves by meaning — "that comparison of static site hosts" — so you don't need to remember which tab, which window, or which day. Compare that to scanning forty favicon slivers looking for the right one. For how this works in practice, see [how to search bookmarks with AI](/blog/how-to-search-bookmarks-with-ai).

## Keep a session together

If the tabs belonged to one project — trip planning, a purchase decision, competitor research — group the saves into a [board](/faq/what-are-boards) after capturing them. The session stays intact as a collection instead of dissolving into your general library, and you can pick the project back up exactly where the tabs left off.
