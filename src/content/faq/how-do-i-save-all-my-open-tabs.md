Use Marqly's tab saver to save all your open tabs at once. Every tab lands in your Marqly library in a single action — no bookmarking them one by one. A 30-tab research session becomes a two-second job, and the window can finally close.

## Tab hoarding is a filing problem

Tabs stay open for one reason: closing them feels like losing them, and saving them properly — one at a time, each into the right folder — takes longer than anyone will actually spend. The tab saver removes the capture half of the problem. The filing half is handled by [AI auto-tagging](/faq/how-does-ai-auto-tagging-work), a Pro feature that tags your saves automatically, so there's no organizing debt waiting on the other side of the click.

## Closed, but not lost

Once saved, those pages are more findable than they ever were as tabs. On Pro, [semantic search](/faq/how-do-i-search-my-bookmarks-by-meaning) retrieves saves by meaning — "that comparison of static site hosts" — so you don't need to remember which tab, which window, or which day. On the free plan, keyword search still covers your entire library, so the captured tabs remain findable by exact words. Compare either of those to scanning forty favicon slivers looking for the right one. For how retrieval works in practice, see [how to search bookmarks with AI](/blog/how-to-search-bookmarks-with-ai).

## Close the whole set, reopen it later

If the window is a working session rather than material to file, tab sessions go one step further: save and close your current set of tabs in one action, reopen the session later to get the exact set back, and undo a close if you cleared something you didn't mean to. The saver turns the pages into lasting library items; the session keeps the working state. The [tab manager page](/tab-manager) walks through that workflow.

## Keep a session together

If the tabs belonged to one project — trip planning, a purchase decision, competitor research — group the saves into a [board](/faq/what-are-boards) after capturing them. The session stays intact as a collection instead of dissolving into your general library, and you can pick the project back up exactly where the tabs left off.

## The honest limits

Two things to know before you make this a habit. The tab saver and sessions work on every plan, but AI auto-tagging and semantic search are Pro — see [free vs Pro](/faq/whats-in-marqly-free-vs-pro). And the free plan stores up to 100 bookmarks (all readable and keyword-searchable), so a daily 30-tab dump fills that headroom quickly; heavy tab-hoarders should weigh [Pro](/pricing) before hitting the ceiling by surprise.
