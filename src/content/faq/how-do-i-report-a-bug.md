Email support@marqly.com with a description of what went wrong and the steps to reproduce it. The most useful reports answer three questions: what you did, what you expected, and what actually happened. If the bug is in the browser extension, include which browser you're on and the extension version — the same issue can behave differently across Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.

## What makes a report actionable

A bug that can be reproduced is a bug that gets fixed. Include:

- **Steps to reproduce** — numbered, starting from a fresh page. "Open a YouTube video, click the bookmark button, switch tabs before it finishes" beats "saving is broken."
- **Expected vs actual** — what you thought would happen, and what did.
- **Where it happened** — the URL of the page, if the bug involves saving, highlighting, or the YouTube card on a specific site.
- **Your setup** — browser and version, and the Marqly extension version.
- **A screenshot or screen recording** — worth more than any paragraph.

## Finding your extension version

Your browser's extensions page lists the version number next to each installed extension: `chrome://extensions` in Chrome, `edge://extensions` in Edge, `about:addons` in Firefox, and Settings → Extensions in Safari. Copy the number for Marqly into your email. If you're several versions behind, updating (or reinstalling from [the store listing](/faq/how-do-i-install-the-chrome-extension)) is worth trying first — the fix may already have shipped.

## What happens next

Reports go straight to the team behind the product — see [how to contact support](/faq/how-do-i-contact-support) for the full picture of what support handles. Before writing, a quick check of the help center at [help.marqly.com](https://help.marqly.com) sometimes resolves things faster: what looks like a bug is occasionally a setting or a known quirk with a documented workaround.
