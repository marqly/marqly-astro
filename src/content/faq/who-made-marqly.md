Marqly is built by Marqly Labs, a small independent team. It's a focused product — an AI bookmark manager spanning browser extensions for Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari, a web app, and an iOS app — not a side project inside a larger company. The team ships under the Marqly Labs name, and you can reach the people who build it directly at support@marqly.com.

## A small team, one product

Marqly Labs works on Marqly and nothing else. That focus shows up in the product's scope: saving, AI auto-tagging, summaries, semantic search, highlighting, and boards — the tools for keeping what you find on the web and actually finding it again. There's a free tier you can try without a credit card, and a Pro plan for heavier use; see [how much does Marqly cost](/faq/how-much-does-marqly-cost) for current pricing.

## Track record so far

You don't have to take the team's word for the product quality:

- A #1 Product of the Day launch on Product Hunt
- ★ 4.7 rating on Product Hunt

Those are current ratings from real users of the extension and the app, not launch-day snapshots.
