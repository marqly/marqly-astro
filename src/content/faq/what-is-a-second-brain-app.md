A second brain app is software for capturing and retrieving the information you would otherwise forget — articles, videos, highlights, notes, and ideas. Instead of trying to remember what you read, you save it to a trusted external system and let that system do the remembering. Two things define a good one: capture is instant, and retrieval works even when you've forgotten the title.
