Yes, and you don't need to ask anyone. In the app's sidebar menu (under Import/Export), **Export** downloads your bookmarks as a CSV file. The free plan exports your **100 most recent** bookmarks; **Pro** exports the **whole library** and can export **only what's new since your last export** — handy if you keep a running backup outside Marqly.

## What's in the file

The CSV carries four columns: **URL, Title, Description, and Tags**. That's a real, portable bookmark file — it imports cleanly into most bookmark managers, including the same browser-HTML/CSV ecosystem Marqly imports from. It is deliberately not an archive: highlights, notes, and board membership stay in the app, and items keep their current titles rather than snapshots.

## If you need everything, not just the CSV

For a **complete copy of your account** — bookmarks plus boards, highlights, notes, and the rest — email **support@marqly.com** from the address on your account and a human will send it. Requesting it changes nothing about your account; it's a copy, not a withdrawal.

## Why lock-in is the thing to check before you commit

The real cost of a bookmark manager isn't the subscription — it's the years of curation you'd lose if you couldn't leave. Marqly is built on the other side of that trade: it imports from Pocket, Raindrop.io, and standard browser bookmark exports precisely because people deserve to move *in* freely, and the same logic applies on the way out. If you've been through a forced migration before — the Pocket shutdown left millions scrambling, as covered in [how to export and migrate your Pocket data](/blog/how-to-export-migrate-pocket-data) — you already know why this matters.

## Common reasons to export

- **Peace of mind.** A periodic backup of a library you've spent years building — Pro's "new since last export" makes this a 10-second habit.
- **Downgrading or leaving.** Note that [canceling Pro doesn't delete anything](/faq/what-happens-to-my-bookmarks-if-i-cancel) — your saves stay accessible on the free tier — so exporting is optional, not urgent.
- **Closing your account.** If you're deleting your account, export *first*, because [deletion removes your saves](/faq/how-do-i-delete-my-account) permanently.

For how your data is handled while it's in Marqly, see the [privacy policy](/privacypolicy).
