Your Marqly data — bookmarks, tags, highlights, and notes — is stored in secure cloud infrastructure, tied to your private account. Cloud storage is what makes the product work: your library syncs across your devices, so a save made in Chrome on your laptop is instantly searchable from the web app or the iOS app. The [privacy policy](/privacypolicy) describes what's stored and how it's handled.

## Why cloud storage, not local files

A bookmark manager that only lives on one machine fails at its main job. Because your library lives in your account rather than on a single device:

- Every save is available everywhere you sign in — extension, web app at [app.marqly.com](https://app.marqly.com), and iOS.
- Losing or replacing a device doesn't lose your library.
- AI features — auto-tagging, summaries, semantic search — run against your whole library, not just what happens to be on the current machine.

Note that Marqly has no offline reading mode; your library is accessed through your signed-in devices.

## Who can see it

Only you, through your account. Saves are private by default; the single exception is a board you deliberately share as a public page, which is an explicit action on your part. Marqly's business model is Pro subscriptions, not advertising, so your data isn't the product — [does Marqly sell my data](/faq/does-marqly-sell-my-data) goes deeper on that.

## Your data stays portable

Stored in the cloud doesn't mean locked in the cloud. You can request a full export of your data by emailing **support@marqly.com** — see [can I export my data](/faq/can-i-export-my-data) — and you can delete your account and its data entirely whenever you choose. For the formal version of all of this, the privacy policy is the authoritative document; if you have a question it doesn't answer, support@marqly.com will.
