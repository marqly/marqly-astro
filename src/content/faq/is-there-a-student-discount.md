Yes. Verified students pay **$48 for their first year** of Marqly Pro instead of $72 — a saving of $24. After that first year it renews at the standard annual price, and you can re-verify if you're still studying.

## How to get it

1. Go to [checkout](https://app.marqly.com/checkout?billing=yearly) and choose the yearly plan.
2. In the **Student discount** panel, enter your university email address. It can be different from the email on your Marqly account — most people sign up with a personal address.
3. We email a 6-digit code to that address. Enter it, and your discount is applied automatically.

The code is single-use and tied to your account, and each university email can only be used once.

## If your university email isn't recognised

We recognise addresses ending in `.edu`, plus academic domains under country codes such as `ac.uk`, `edu.au` and `ac.jp`. Plenty of real universities — especially in Europe — use ordinary-looking domains that no automatic check can spot.

If yours isn't recognised, **email support@marqly.com with proof of enrolment** and we'll sort it out manually. You are not excluded; the automatic check just can't confirm every institution.

## Worth knowing before you use it

- The discount applies to the **yearly** plan only.
- Using a discount code starts your subscription immediately, **without the free trial**. If you'd rather try Pro first, start the trial and upgrade afterwards.
- It covers your first year. Renewal is at the standard price unless you re-verify.

## The other ways to pay less

- **The free tier.** Save, auto-tag and search with no card and no time limit. For a student collecting research links and lecture resources, it may be all you need — see [is Marqly free](/faq/is-marqly-free).
- **Annual billing.** $72/yr versus $108/yr paid monthly — the built-in ~33% saving covered in [coupons and discounts](/faq/marqly-coupons-and-discounts).

## Why $6/month is already student-friendly

At the annual rate, Marqly costs about the same per month as a coffee — and with the student discount, less. The features that matter for studying — AI summaries to triage a reading list, semantic search to find "that paper about spaced repetition" without remembering the title, and chatting with long lecture videos on YouTube — are the kind of thing that saves hours during exam season.
