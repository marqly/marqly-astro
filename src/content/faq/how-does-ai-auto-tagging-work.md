Every time you save something to Marqly, AI tags it automatically — you never file anything by hand. Save an article, a video, or a link, and relevant tags are applied at save time with zero input from you. Those tags are searchable in your library, so the organizing work that folder-based bookmark systems demand (and that everyone eventually abandons) simply doesn't exist in Marqly.

## No manual filing, by design

Traditional bookmark managers make organization your job: create folders, name them, decide where each save goes, and keep the system tidy forever. The system works exactly as long as your discipline does. Marqly inverts that — tagging happens automatically on every save, whether it's a single one-click save from the toolbar or a whole batch from the tab saver. The cost of saving something drops to one click, which means you actually save things.

## Tags are for finding, not just filing

Auto-applied tags are searchable across your library, so they act as ready-made entry points: pull up everything tagged around a topic without ever having curated that topic yourself. And tags are only one layer of retrieval. Marqly's semantic search matches meaning across titles, content, highlights, and transcripts — so even a save whose tags you'd never guess is still findable by describing it. How the two layers fit together is covered in [what is semantic search](/faq/what-is-semantic-search) and in [how to search your bookmarks with AI](/blog/how-to-search-bookmarks-with-ai).

## It works on your backlog too

Auto-tagging isn't just for new saves. When you import existing collections — Chrome/Firefox/Edge/Safari bookmark HTML exports, Pocket export files, or Raindrop.io collections — AI tags the entire import automatically. A decade of unfiled browser bookmarks comes out the other side organized, with no sorting session required. See [how do I import Chrome bookmarks](/faq/how-do-i-import-chrome-bookmarks) for that flow.

## Try it on your own saves

The fastest way to evaluate auto-tagging is with your own messy bookmarks rather than a demo. [Try Marqly free](https://app.marqly.com) — no credit card required — import what you have, and see what the tags look like on your real library.
